About War Thunder

War Thunder is an online military vehicle combat game developed by Gaijin Entertainment. It brings aircraft, ground vehicles, helicopters and naval forces into battles inspired by different eras of military technology.

Players can progress through vehicle trees, customize machines and take part in different combat modes. The official game is free to play, with optional in-game purchases available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about War Thunder.

Is War Thunder free to play?

Yes. War Thunder is officially presented as a free-to-play online military vehicles game, with optional in-game purchases.

Which platforms support War Thunder?

The official desktop requirements cover Windows, macOS and Linux. The game is also available on consoles.

What is the latest major War Thunder update?

The official changelog currently highlights the Sky Odyssey major update, released on September 16, 2026.

Can tanks and aircraft fight together?

Yes. In combined battles, ground vehicles and aircraft or helicopters can participate together. Naval battles can also combine ships and aircraft.

How much storage does War Thunder need?

On Windows, the official requirements list 23.1 GB for the minimal client and 75.9 GB for the full client.

Where should I download War Thunder?

Use the official War Thunder website to download the current client for your supported platform.
